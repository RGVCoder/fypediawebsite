# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Learners who discover educational content in short-form video and want a clearer path from watching to understanding. They are choosing a topic, protecting a small amount of time, and looking for an approachable starting point rather than a full course catalog.

## Product Purpose

Fypedia turns educational Shorts into focused learning sessions: a learner chooses topics, watches a small set of clips, and answers knowledge checks that help turn attention into understanding. Success means a newcomer can quickly choose a starting topic and complete a useful session.

## Positioning

Fypedia connects short-form discovery with a lightweight learning loop: watch a short, answer one grounded question, and keep a record of what you are learning. It is not a claim that short-form video replaces a full course or textbook.

## Operating Context

The product is used in a browser, often in short sessions. The first-run workflow is email or Google authentication, topic selection, and a daily time goal. The main product workflow is a personal study feed with topic management, focused sessions, knowledge checks, results, and local learning statistics.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- New accounts verify their email or use Google sign-in before entering the product.
- Learners choose at least three topics during onboarding and can add custom topics later.
- Live sessions use YouTube videos and transcript-grounded question generation through InsForge functions.
- The browser bundle contains only public InsForge configuration; provider and content-service credentials stay server-side.
- No testimonials, customer logos, usage statistics, or outcome guarantees are available to use as proof.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Fypedia. The existing interface uses a dark charcoal base, warm paper and amber accents, serif display headings, and calm, editorial language. The landing page should feel like the beginning of the same study tool, not a separate campaign site.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the working browser app, Fypedia logo assets in `dist/assets/`, topic descriptions and images in `dist/app.js`, and existing auth, onboarding, study-session, library, profile, and results flows. There are no approved testimonials or external proof assets.

## Product Principles

- Make starting small feel worthwhile.
- Show the learning loop instead of overpromising an outcome.
- Let curiosity choose the topic.
- Keep each session focused and easy to resume.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience should remain keyboard accessible, use visible focus states, preserve readable contrast, provide text alternatives for meaningful imagery, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
